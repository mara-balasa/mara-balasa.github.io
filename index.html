PhD Candidate, Stockholm School of Economics
Visiting Researcher, Cambridge Judge Business School
Senior Associate, Energy Policy Group

My research examines how the cost structures and market maturity of hard-to-abate industries such as steel, ammonia and methanol shape the economics of decarbonisation and the design of industrial policy instruments. Moreover, I am exploring how energy bills for heavy industry are structured across the UK and several EU countries.

I have previous work experience at the European Central Bank, the World Bank Group, the Permanent Representation of Romania to the European Union and the Romanian Ministry of Economy.
