I am a PhD candidate at the Center for Statecraft and Strategic Communication at the Stockholm School of Economics, and a visiting researcher at the Energy Policy Research Group at Cambridge Judge Business School.

My research examines how the costs and market structure of hard-to-abate industires such as steel, ammonia and methanol shape the challenges of decarbonising them and the design of policy instruments meant to support the transition.

Previous work experience includes: European Central Bank, World Bank Group, Permanent Representation of Romania to the European Union and Romanian Ministry of Economy.
